Project Overview

Assessment policy

Risk = Likelihood (1–5) × Impact (1–5). Low: 1–4; Medium: 5–9; High: 10–14; Critical: 15–25. These are configurable-policy candidates, not a universal risk standard. Treatment completion does not automatically lower a risk score; reassess the risk using evidence.

Academic use: instructors can distribute this static tool and students can export a risk register or JSON project file for submission.
